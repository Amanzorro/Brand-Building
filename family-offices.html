Family offices

One view of the family's wealth, in the family's language.

Reporting

Consolidation across custodians

Statements from private banks, fund administrators and direct holdings are pulled into one governed view. Monthly reporting is drafted automatically and reconciled against source.

Governance

Family council & board packs

Bilingual packs cover performance, liquidity, upcoming capital calls and decisions needed, written for both the principals and the next generation.

Directs

Direct-deal screening

Inbound co-investments and club deals are screened against the family's investment policy statement, values and Sharia requirements before anyone takes a meeting.

Structure

Entity & document intelligence

A searchable map of holding structures, foundations, trusts and SPVs across DIFC, ADGM and other jurisdictions, with key terms extracted from every agreement.

Continuity

Institutional memory

Why the family made each decision is written down and kept, so the reasoning survives staff changes and is there for the next generation.

Privacy

Discretion by architecture

Deployments are single-tenant with no shared infrastructure. Access is granted per family member and per adviser, and every view is logged.

Sovereign & government-related investors

Support for teams that manage national capital.

Sovereign and GRE investment teams operate under strict mandate, procurement and data-sovereignty requirements. We work within them: on your infrastructure, under your policies and through your vendor processes.

Mandates

Mandate & policy alignment checks

Every proposed investment is checked against the governing mandate, strategic priorities and exclusion lists, with a clear audit trail for the board.

Co-investment

Partner & co-investment screening

A consistent first read on GP proposals, co-investment offers and joint-venture documentation, in your template and in both languages.

Research

Sector & country briefings

Briefings on sectors, countries and counterparties are assembled from your approved sources, in English or Arabic, for leadership and board audiences.

Infrastructure
