Industry · Exporters · Energy · Logistics

From invoices to a verified inventory, without the spreadsheet relay.

The UAE Climate Law requires organisations to measure and report their emissions, and the EU's carbon border mechanism now charges for the emissions embedded in some exports. Nokhada builds the inventory, the reporting packs and the CBAM data from the documents you already have.

The problem

The obligation is new. The data is scattered across the business.

  • Evidence in a dozen formats

    Electricity, water and cooling bills from different providers, fuel-card exports, generator logs and refrigerant top-ups. Some arrive as PDFs, some as scans, many in Arabic.

  • One sustainability lead, many sites

    Climate reporting often falls on one or two people who chase site managers by email, rebuild spreadsheets each year and answer verifier queries from memory.

  • Customers now ask for numbers too

    EU importers need installation-level embedded-emissions data. Multinational customers send Scope 3 questionnaires. Each request arrives in its own format.

Workflows

Built around your reporting cycle.

Each workflow reads your methodology files first: boundary, factors, estimation rules and materiality. Results are consistent year on year and from one site to the next.

01

Scope 1 & 2 inventory from evidence

Claude Code reads every invoice and log, extracts activity data and applies your chosen factors. The calculation is committed as code that can be rerun. Gaps are filled using your documented estimation rules and flagged as estimates.

Today

Someone types bill totals into a spreadsheet. Formulas break, estimates go unlabelled and last year's logic lives in someone's head.

With Nokhada

Every line traces to a document. Estimates are labelled with the rule that produced them, and next year's run is the same code with new evidence.

02

Climate Law & NRCC reporting packs

Your reporting submission is drafted in the required structure, with an evidence index cross-referencing every figure. Before it leaves the building, the Sieve checks it.

Today

Verifier sampling turns into a hunt through shared drives, and queries take days to answer.

With Nokhada

The verifier gets an evidence index, and queries are answered from the same repository in minutes.

03

CBAM embedded emissions

For aluminium, steel, cement, fertiliser and other covered goods, it calculates direct and indirect embedded emissions by installation and product, drafts the communication template for EU importers and keeps the supporting evidence ready for review.

04

Scope 3 & supplier data

It sends supplier questionnaires in Arabic and English, reads the replies however they come back (spreadsheets, PDFs or email text), scores data quality and falls back to spend-based estimates where suppliers can't answer.

05

Customer questionnaires

Climate questionnaires from customers, lenders and rating platforms are answered in their own formats from your governed inventory, so every answer matches what you reported.

06

Decarbonisation planning

It models abatement options site by site, including efficiency, on-site solar, cooling upgrades, fuel switching and procurement, then drafts a reduction plan for the board with assumptions stated.
