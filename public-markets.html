Workflows

From signal to sized position, with a human making every call.

Claude Code does the reading, structuring and challenging. Your PMs decide. Nothing is executed and no order is placed. The system ends at research output.

Stage 1 · Breadth

Signal sweeps across the universe

Each night, your screening framework runs across every name in coverage, covering filings, transcripts, exchange disclosures and Arabic press. Candidates come back with the evidence attached.

Stage 2 · Depth

The Majlis Test

Each surviving thesis is broken into its load-bearing assumptions, and each assumption is attacked with specific evidence. A thesis either Holds or Sinks. There is no "it depends".

Stage 3 · Output

Models, notes & trade maps

For ideas the PM backs, it builds the model update, the research note in house format and the catalyst calendar, all versioned in your repository.

Daily

Pre-market brief for the Gulf day

A brief timed to your desk's hours covers overnight US and Asia moves, GCC exchange announcements, central-bank statements and what changed for each position.

Earnings

Bilingual earnings coverage

Results from ADX, DFM, Tadawul and other GCC venues are read in whichever language they're published in and compared against your model. Differences between Arabic and English versions are flagged.

Memory

A thesis archive that learns

Every thesis is stored with its verdict, its outcome and what you learned from it. Before each new task, Claude reads what your firm got wrong last time.

Controls

Designed for a regulated research desk.

  • Information barriers respected

    Connectors are scoped per team and per user. If a pod can't see a dataset today, it can't see it through Claude either.

  • Full audit trail

    Every prompt, file read, source and output is logged for compliance review under DFSA or FSRA record-keeping expectations.

  • No execution, ever

    The system stops at research and has no connection to order-management or execution systems. Every decision is made by a person.
