Services

The same system, set up for three kinds of organisation. It runs in your own environment, and you keep the code and method files.

Industry and exporters

The UAE Climate Law requires organisations to measure and report their greenhouse-gas emissions. EU importers of aluminium, iron and steel, cement and fertilisers now need emissions data from their suppliers.

  • An emissions inventory built from your bills, fuel records and refrigerant logs, in Arabic or English
  • Reporting packs for the Climate Law and the NRCC, with an index of source documents for your verifier
  • CBAM embedded-emissions data by installation and product
  • Answers to customer and lender questionnaires, drawn from the same figures

Banks, insurers and investors

UAE financial regulators expect licensed firms to manage climate risk, disclose it and plan their transition.

  • Financed emissions for loan and investment books
  • Physical-risk screening of collateral and investee assets
  • Draft transition plans and disclosures, built from the data your risk team already uses
  • Method documents and run logs that your model-risk and audit teams can review

Real estate and infrastructure

Heat, cooling demand, water supply and occasional flooding drive the climate exposure of UAE buildings.

  • A risk register for each asset covering heat, flood, coastal and water exposure
  • Energy, water and district-cooling data normalised per asset
  • Landlord and tenant emissions reported per fund or entity
  • Supporting documents for local green-building regulations
