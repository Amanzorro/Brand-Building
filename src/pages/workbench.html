Reporting period and organisation

CSV columns
site, account_id, document_ref
Where the activity happened, the meter or account, and the invoice or note number.
source_type
One of electricity, district_cooling, diesel, petrol, generator_diesel, refrigerant or water.
refrigerant_type
Needed for refrigerant rows, for example R-410A.
period_start, period_end
YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY. Arabic digits are accepted.
quantity, unit
Units include kWh, MWh, RTh, L, m3, US gal, imp gal, kg and t.
