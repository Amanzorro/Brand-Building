Controls

Standard in every deployment.

  • Identity & access

    Sign-on through your identity provider. Role-based access mirrors your existing entitlements.

  • Least-privilege connectors

    Connectors are read-only by default, and write access is granted per workflow with an approval step. Credentials stay in your secrets manager.

  • Complete audit logs

    Every prompt, file access, tool call and output is logged to your SIEM and kept for your required retention period.

  • Training on your data

    We configure deployments under commercial API terms. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, customer inputs and outputs aren't used to train models by default. We walk your team through the terms for the route you choose.

  • Humans decide

    Every workflow ends with a named person approving the output. Nothing reaches a regulator, a verifier or a customer without review.
