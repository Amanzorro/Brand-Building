Workflows

From counterparty data to board-ready output.

Claude Code reads, calculates and drafts. Your risk and credit teams decide. Nothing changes a credit decision or a limit without a person approving it.

Measure

Financed & insured emissions

Emissions are attributed to loans and investments using your chosen method, with data-quality scores and every estimate labelled with its source.

Assess

Physical-risk screening

Collateral and investee assets are geocoded and screened against heat, flood, coastal and water-stress layers. Concentrations are flagged for the risk committee.

Assess

Transition-risk review

Counterparty transition plans, disclosures and sector exposure are read and compared with your sector policies, and the gaps feed into your client engagement.

Plan

Transition planning

Your institution's transition plan is drafted from governed data, structured to the principles UAE financial regulators have adopted.

Finance

Green & sustainability-linked deals

Use-of-proceeds eligibility and KPI checks run against your green finance framework, covering green bonds, green sukuk and sustainability-linked loans, with allocation and impact reporting drafted.

Disclose

Disclosures & regulator returns

Climate disclosures aligned with the ISSB standards, plus board and supervisory packs, are drafted bilingually from the same data your risk teams use.

Controls

Designed for a regulated institution.

  • Customer data stays protected

    Deployment is in your tenancy, under your entitlements and banking-secrecy controls. Connectors are scoped per team.

  • Model-risk friendly

    Methodology is written down, calculations are code and every run is logged. Your model-risk and internal-audit teams can review it like any other model.

  • People decide

    The system informs credit and risk decisions and never makes them. Every output carries a named approver.
