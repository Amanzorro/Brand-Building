What we do

Emissions inventory
Scope 1 and 2, and Scope 3 where you need it, calculated from your invoices, meter readings and fuel records, with each figure linked to its source document.
Climate Law, NRCC and CBAM reporting
Reporting packs for the UAE Climate Law and the National Register for Carbon Credits, plus embedded-emissions data for goods you export to the EU.
Climate risk
Heat, flood and water exposure for buildings and portfolios, and transition risk for lenders and investors.

How it works

  1. Two-week diagnostic. We map your obligations and data, run one site end to end and give you a written plan.
  2. Build. We set up the system in your own cloud account or servers and connect it to your records.
  3. Run. Your team produces each report. We stay on a monthly retainer for changes.
