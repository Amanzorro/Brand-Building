Developers · Owners · Operators · Infrastructure

Know what the climate means for every asset you own.

In the UAE, buildings and infrastructure carry the country's biggest climate exposures: heat, cooling load, water and the occasional severe flood. Nokhada turns your asset registers, meter data and building documents into asset-level risk, performance and reporting.

Workflows

Asset by asset, portfolio by portfolio.

Risk

Physical-risk register

Every asset is screened for extreme heat, flooding, coastal exposure and water stress. The result is a register your board, lenders and insurers can read.

Operate

Energy, water & cooling

Utility, district-cooling and meter data is normalised per asset. It's compared against similar buildings, with the outliers and their likely causes flagged.

Report

Portfolio emissions

Landlord and tenant emissions are separated and estimated where data is missing, then reported consistently across the portfolio and for each fund or entity.

Certify

Green-building documentation

Evidence for local green-building regulations and rating systems is assembled from design and operational documents, with gaps flagged before submission.

Invest

Acquisition due diligence

For each target asset, it reads the climate exposure, energy performance and retrofit needs from the data room and writes them into the investment case.

Adapt

Adaptation & retrofit plans

Cooling upgrades, shading, drainage, solar and water reuse are prioritised against cost, risk reduction and tenant impact, then drafted as a capex plan.

Why it matters here

Rare events still shape the risk.

The record rainfall of April 2024 showed that a country built for heat can also be badly disrupted by water. Insurers, lenders and buyers now ask about both. An asset-level register, kept up to date and backed by evidence, turns those questions from a scramble into a lookup.
