What to expect

From first call to a system your team runs.

No surprises: here is what happens at each stage, what we'll ask of you and what you'll have at the end of it.

Day 0

First conversation

A 45-minute call about your obligations and your data. We'll sign an NDA first if you'd like.

From you: someone who knows where the bills and logs live.

Week 1

Diagnostic: map

Your obligations map for each entity and site, an inventory of your data sources and owners, and the gaps.

From you: a few interviews and sample documents.

Week 2

Diagnostic: prove

We run one site, product line or portfolio slice end to end on your real documents, including the Sieve. Then you get the written plan and a fixed-fee build proposal.

From you: read access to one site's records.

Weeks 3–8

Build

Methodology files, connectors and calculations go into your environment, and every site comes on stream. Weekly demos on your own data.

From you: a named owner, an IT security contact and a few hours a week of your team's time.

Ongoing

Run

Your team runs each reporting cycle. We stay on a monthly retainer for new rules, new sites and verifier queries.

From you: sign-off on what gets submitted.

What you'll get

  • An obligations map covering every entity and site
  • An emissions inventory where every figure traces to a document
  • An evidence index your verifier can sample from
  • Methodology files and calculation code in your own repository
  • A review queue of everything the Sieve caught
  • Board and regulator summaries in Arabic and English
  • Training and a handover your team can run without us

What we won't do

  • Verify our own work. We stay independent of your verifier.
  • Take your data out of your environment
  • Submit anything without your sign-off
  • Give legal advice. We work alongside your counsel.
  • Charge per seat or per site

How pricing works

  • Diagnostic: fixed fee, two weeks
  • Build: fixed fee, agreed at the end of the diagnostic
  • Run: a monthly retainer you can stop at any time
  • Model usage: billed at cost, with no markup
