The premise

The hard part of climate work isn't the software. It's your evidence.

  • The data is in documents, not databases

    Emissions live in utility bills, fuel receipts, district-cooling invoices, supplier emails and maintenance logs, many of them in Arabic. Most carbon software expects someone to type them in.

  • Methodology is hidden in vendor defaults

    Boundaries, emission factors and estimation rules end up as settings in someone else's product. When a verifier asks why a number is what it is, the answer is hard to show.

  • The Gulf climate is its own case

    Cooling dominates energy use, and district cooling, desalinated water, extreme heat and rare but severe flooding all matter here. Global templates treat these as edge cases, but in the UAE they're central.

UAE climate rules →

  • Fluent in UAE climate regulation

    We follow the Federal Climate Law and the NRCC, the climate-risk and disclosure principles adopted by the CBUAE, CMA, DFSA and FSRA, and emirate-level rules, and we track changes as they're issued.

  • Arabic evidence, read natively

    Utility and cooling bills, permits, contracts and supplier responses are read in the language they were issued in, with no data-entry queue.

  • Methodology tuned to the Gulf

    District cooling, desalinated water, on-site solar and heat-driven load profiles are handled explicitly. Emission factors are chosen, cited and versioned, never assumed.

  • Operational data stays in-house

    Energy, production and supplier data is commercially sensitive. We deploy in your tenancy and document every data flow, including where model inference runs.
