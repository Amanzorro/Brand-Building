The problem

A lean team, a crowded pipeline and an IC that wants more.

  • Too many teasers, too few hours

    Sovereign co-investment, family-office directs and a busy GCC IPO market all add to the inbound. Associates spend their weeks triaging deals instead of analysing them.

  • Data rooms in two languages

    Commercial registrations, municipality permits, court records and management accounts often arrive in Arabic, and English-only tools either skip them or garble them.

  • Portfolio reporting eats the quarter

    Each portfolio company reports in its own format. Ops teams rebuild the same pack by hand every quarter for LPs who increasingly ask for more.

Workflows

Built around your deal cycle, from first teaser to exit.

Each workflow reads your firm's reference files first: thesis, criteria, red flags, template and tone. Results are consistent across associates and improve as your frameworks change.

01

Thesis-driven screening

Every teaser, CIM and broker email is scored against your thesis, with the reasoning written out. Kills are logged with their reason, so you can come back to them when your thesis changes.

Today

Associates skim every inbound, keep notes in personal spreadsheets and apply criteria inconsistently. Good deals get missed on busy weeks.

With Nokhada

Every inbound gets the same first read against your criteria. Associates start from a ranked shortlist with citations and spend their time on the deals that deserve it.

02

Diligence across both languages

Claude Code reads the whole data room, Arabic and English documents together. It reconciles management accounts against audited statements, flags related-party exposure and builds a question list for management.

Today

Arabic documents queue for an outside translator. Cross-checking happens late, under deadline, if it happens at all.

With Nokhada

Both languages are read on the first pass. Inconsistencies come up early, each one linked to the page it came from.

03

IC memos in your template

A first draft arrives in your committee's exact structure, with every number traced to its source. An Arabic version can follow for board members or co-investors who prefer it. Before the memo goes to committee, it runs through the Majlis Test.

Today

Two analysts spend a week formatting, cross-referencing and rewriting. Senior time goes on proofreading, not judgement.

With Nokhada

Analysts edit and challenge a cited draft. Seniors review the thesis rather than the formatting.

04

Portfolio monitoring & LP reporting

Monthly packs from each portfolio company are normalised, compared with plan and summarised. Quarterly LP letters and ILPA-style templates are drafted from the same governed data.

Today

Ops rebuild the same pack from a dozen formats every quarter, and variances get noticed late.

With Nokhada

Variances are flagged the day data lands, and the LP letter starts as a draft rather than a blank page.

05

Real estate underwriting

It pulls comparable transactions, rental indices, service-charge history and off-plan payment schedules into your underwriting model, then writes the investment case in your format.

06

Sharia-compliant structuring support

For Islamic funds and mandates, it checks targets against your board's screening methodology and summarises sukuk and murabaha documentation for your scholars. Purification amounts are calculated for their review. The output informs the Sharia Supervisory Board and does not replace it.
