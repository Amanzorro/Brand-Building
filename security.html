Security and data protection

Your documents, code and logs stay in your systems.

Where it runs

We deploy in your own cloud account (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, in a UAE region if you need one) or on your own servers. We store nothing by default.

Claude models are available through Anthropic's API and through cloud platforms such as Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud Vertex AI. Regional availability varies, so before go-live we agree with your compliance team where prompts and outputs are processed.

Data-protection rules

  • UAE Federal Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021)
  • DIFC Data Protection Law (DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020)
  • ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021
  • Outsourcing and technology-risk rules of the CBUAE, CMA, DFSA and FSRA, where they apply

We are not a law firm. Your counsel decides whether a deployment meets your obligations.

Controls

  • Sign-in through your identity provider
  • Access that matches your existing permissions
  • Connectors read-only by default
  • Every prompt, file access and output logged to your systems
  • A named person approves every output before it leaves your organisation
